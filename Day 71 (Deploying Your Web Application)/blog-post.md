Ah, 'twas a simpler time, the '80s! We had bands like Baltimora, pumping out hits like Tarzan Boy. Wait, how do I know this? I wasn't even born in the '80s! Luckily, Apple Music exists.

I've been on a bit of an Italo disco binge lately, playing Sandy Morton's Camel by Camel on repeat, and listening to Woody Boogie and Tarzan Boy by Baltimora like my life depends on it. This inspired me to create an Italo disco remix of lunaluxe's iconic track Never Looked So Good.

The basic premise was simple enough: use a simple Italo disco drum beat with a catchy synth, and fit lunaluxe's track to it. Thankfully, the track itself was pretty amenable to this, being 105 BPM and D Maj. I only had to shift the track up by 2 BPM to match the BPM of Baltimora's iconic Tarzan Boy. The rest was pretty much taken care of by Splice. I created a sick tom intro using one-shots from Splice, and then used the intro as a loop sparingly throughout the track.

As for the synth, this is where things get a little more complicated. There was no Italo disco synth at D Maj, so I had to pick one at C Maj, and shift it up by 2 semitones in Ableton Live. For the bass I elected to go with a sort of muted nu disco bass, to which I added a copious amount of OTT distortion. To round it all off, I added a rhythm electric guitar. Finally, for the vocals I used my own FX rack, called "Retro lo-fi vox", modified for this track specifically. I automated the chorus on the reverb section to be switched on whenever the track's hook dropped.

Seems simple enough, right? All hail Splice!
